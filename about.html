About this book

Frog and Toad and the Increasingly Capable Machines is written by Elizabeth Van Nostrand. It is a satire of Arnold Lobel's Frog and Toad books and has no connection or endorsement from Lobel's estate or publishers.

This book was illustrated by HungerArtist who despite his fake-sounding pseudonym is a human with decades of experience in illustrations. If you're interested in hiring him, Elizabeth has permission to share his UpWork profile privately.

This book was funded by Timothy Telleen-Lawton. Thanks Timothy!
